How Cube Clash works
1
Pick your events. 4 permanent events: 3x3, 2x2, 4x4, 3x3 OH + 1 Rotating event that changes every week. Enter as many or as few as you like.
2
Check the format on the scramble card. It tells you how many solves that event needs.
3
Grab that event's scrambles (right here, or from the Academy post).
4
Solve them all on any timer. Most events are average of 5: drop your best and worst, average the middle 3.
5
6x6 and 7x7 are mean of 3. Only 3 solves, and you average all three. Nothing gets dropped, so every solve counts.
6
Post your times in the Academy thread before the Monday deadline. Say which event each one is.
7
Champions are crowned Monday night, and the new week starts right after.
